    For tenants

    Good tenants should be worth knowing.

    A tenant gets more than somewhere to pay rent: receipts the moment they pay, a record of how they have paid, and points they earn for paying on time.

    Rental Profile
    A score out of 100 built from payment behaviour only: how often rent cleared on time, how late when late, what is outstanding, the longest unbroken run and length of tenancy. Every point is explained.
    Reward points
    One point for every KES 100 of rent, full rate when paid on time, a bonus for an unbroken streak. Points are held for 30 days before they can be spent.
    Receipts and statements
    Every receipt and the full statement download as PDF, ready to hand to a future landlord.

    Rental Profile Score, not a credit score. It uses only the tenant’s payment history inside RentRewards, and it is not a credit reference bureau product. Redemption of points is coming soon.

    Tenant portal, mobile web. Demonstration tenant. A native tenant app is planned for a later phase.

    Compliance

    Compliance shouldn’t require another spreadsheet.

    RentRewards organises the records that rental income tax filing depends on, so preparing a return starts from data that is already in order.

    1. Property tax profilesEach landlord and property carries its KRA details and taxpayer type.
    2. Rental income recordsConfirmed payments become income records automatically.
    3. Monthly tax periodsRecords roll into a period you can review before anything is sent.
    4. Compliance exceptionsMissing PINs and unmatched payments are flagged for fixing first.
    5. Submission preparationA reviewed period is prepared for filing.
    6. Audit historyWho prepared, changed and submitted what, and when.

    KRA eRITS: the Electronic Rental Income Tax System. RentRewards prepares and organises the records; it does not use any undocumented KRA interface, and in the prototype submissions are simulated and labelled as such.
